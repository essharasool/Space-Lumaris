# Narration scripts (AI voice), FINAL

## 01-intro.mp3
"Look up at the night sky. Past the clouds, past the floating satellites, all the way to the Moon and Mars. It looks quiet, doesn't it? But if you look really closely, you might see the tracks of the greatest relay race in history. A race that took decades, where the runners were made of metal, wire, and dreams. Our story begins not with a starting pistol, but with a rocket."

## 02-surveyor.mp3
"Think of space like a giant, dark garden. Before humans could visit, we needed to know if the soil was safe. In 1967, a brave little scout named Surveyor 3 landed on the Moon. It didn't have wheels to run; it just sat quietly, digging a tiny trench in the dirt. It was planting one of the very first seeds of our cosmic garden, safely passing the baton to the Apollo astronauts who came to visit it two and a half years later."

## 03-sojourner.mp3
"The race didn't stop at the Moon. In 1997, millions of miles away on Mars, a tiny rover named Sojourner rolled out. It was no bigger than a microwave, moving like a slow, careful skateboard. Using its special APXS tool, it sniffed the Martian rocks, tasting the chemistry of an alien world. The baton had made it all the way to Mars!"

## 04-oppy.mp3
"Then came the marathon runner. In 2004, Opportunity bounced onto the red dirt. 'Oppy' was built to run for just 90 days, but it kept going for fourteen years! It used a tough little tool called the RAT to grind into rocks, unlocking secrets about ancient water. But even marathon runners need to rest. When a massive dust storm blocked the sun, Oppy sent its last message home. It wasn't words, just numbers, but the numbers said: no power left, and the sky is dark. The great runner finally went to sleep in the dust."

## 05-outro.mp3
"Some people look at Surveyor, Sojourner, and Opportunity and think they are abandoned. But they aren't lost. They are monuments. They are the finish lines for the next generation of explorers. They wait silently in the dark, holding the baton out, ready for us to come back and run the next lap. The race isn't over. It's just your turn to run."
