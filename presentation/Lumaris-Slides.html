Why we built it

The problem & our idea

The problem

Real NASA mission data is too technical and text-heavy for kids aged 7 to 14.

Our idea

Lumaris turns real exploration into an interactive storybook. Kids follow three NASA robots passing a baton of discovery from the Moon to Mars.

Lumaris · 02

The story

A three-robot cosmic relay

1967

Surveyor 3

Moon · Ocean of Storms

Dug trenches to test the soil before astronauts landed.

1997

Sojourner

Mars · Ares Vallis

First rover on Mars. Planned for 7 days, worked 83.

2004–2018

Opportunity

Mars · Meridiani Planum

Planned for 90 sols, worked over 14 years. Found signs water once soaked the ground.
